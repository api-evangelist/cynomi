Cyber insurance and cyber warranties get blurred together constantly, and the blur serves a purpose: most of the explanations available were written by companies selling warranties. The distinction is simpler than the marketing implies. Cyber insurance is a regulated risk-transfer contract that covers your client’s losses when an incident occurs.
